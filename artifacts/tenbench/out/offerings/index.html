Ask us directly
01What kinds of workspace can I ask about?

You can explore individual work settings, a dedicated meeting room, options for a team, and the lounge. Tell us what kind of workday you have in mind and we can help you understand which spaces might suit it.

02Can I ask about a meeting room?

Yes. The meeting room is a dedicated setting with a long table and presentation screen. Get in touch to discuss your gathering and what you need from the room.

03Do you publish rates or availability here?

No rates or availability are listed on this page. Use the enquiry form to tell us what you are looking for; the form is a preview and does not send or store messages.

04I’m not sure what I need yet. Is that okay?

Absolutely. A rough idea is enough to begin. You might share whether you are working solo, meeting with others, or looking for a team setup.
