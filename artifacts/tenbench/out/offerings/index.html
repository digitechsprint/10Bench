01Can I visit before I decide?

Yes, and we’d encourage it. Call or WhatsApp us to arrange a time, then come and see the desks, cabins and meeting room in person.

Pricing depends on the type of space, the number of seats and how long you need it. Tell us what you’re looking for and we’ll share the options that fit.

Private cabins are designed for teams who need a room of their own. Share your team size and we’ll suggest the right setup.

Absolutely. With a long table and a presentation screen, it’s set up for client meetings, pitches, interviews and team reviews. Get in touch to check availability.
